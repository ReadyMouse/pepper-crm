Contact categories

Reconnect: Never is for people you do not need a timed reminder to reach out to (for example best friends, family, or random acquaintances). They can still show up in Upcoming Birthdays or Random People of the Week, but not in Reconnects Due or travel matches.

Do Not Engage is for contacts you never want Pepper to surface anywhere (for example exes, hookups, or anyone you are keeping in your book for records only). Set this in the contact card CATEGORIES field on your phone.
